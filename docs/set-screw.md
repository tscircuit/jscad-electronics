# SetScrew renderer

`SetScrew`, `createSetScrewGeom` and `createSetScrewMesh` accept modelprinter's exported `SetScrewModelPropsInput`. The renderer consumes `setscrewModelPropsSchema`, preserving its dimensions, units, defaults and validation. Modelprinter owns the pinned ISO 4029:2003 table and documented nominal geometry choices. This older edition is explicit and does not track the 2026 replacement. The React component also accepts a color. Mechanical footprint dispatch returns no electrical pads.

The complete example is `setscrew_standard(iso4029)_m3_l6mm_hexsocket_cuppoint`. The M3 body has a nominal 0.5 mm pitch, 1.5 mm hexagonal socket across-flats, 2 mm blind socket depth and 1.4 mm cup-point contact diameter.

The body, end chamfers and recesses form a single welded, outward-oriented indexed surface. The external thread is a real single-start helix with the nominal flattened 60-degree profile. The point ends in a contact ring surrounding a 120-degree conical cup, and the opposite end has an open hexagonal blind socket with a flat floor. The socket mouth is Z=0, contact ring Z=-length, cup apex Z=-length+cupDepth and socket floor Z=-socketDepth. Right-hand phase, reversed left-hand phase and hidden-thread envelopes follow modelprinter. No coincident internal cylinders or overlapping independent solids are used. Optional socket drill relief, root rounding and socket-mouth rounding are omitted as documented nominal choices.

`radialSegments` defaults to 96, divisible by 24 within 24–192; `threadStepsPerTurn` defaults to 24 within 24–96. More than 400,000 sampled vertices fails before allocation. Geometry tests verify all sizes, opposite edge winding and incidence, connectedness, Euler characteristic, nondegenerate triangles, positive signed volume, actual recess dimensions, cup apex, datums, thread extrema and helical phase. A smooth-body analytic volume check subtracts both blind recesses and validates end clipping. Routing tests compare React, footprint and vanilla bounds/volumes. The standard four-view PNG labels the full string and shows the socket, thread and contact end.
