# HeatSetInsert renderer

`HeatSetInsert`, `createHeatSetInsertGeom` and `createHeatSetInsertMesh` consume modelprinter's exported `HeatSetInsertModelPropsInput` and `heatSetInsertModelPropsSchema`. The component defaults to brass color and accepts a color override. React, footprint and vanilla dispatch share the same geometry; the registration has `pads: "none"`.

The complete string is `heatsetinsert_m3_od4.6mm_l5mm_knurldepth0.2mm_knurlp0.6mm_knurlteeth24_diamondknurl`. The outer crest diameter is 4.6 mm, the root diameter is 4.2 mm, and radial knurl depth is 0.2 mm. The model is an unflanged sleeve with no implicit end bands. It models nominal assembly geometry, not a manufacturer's insert or installation hole specification.

The outer surface consists of crossed helical grooves forming diamond pyramids. Its mathematical phase and radial profile are defined by modelprinter's contract documentation. Circumferential sampling includes every crest and groove at the end datum, and axial sampling includes exact half-pitch rows. The internal bore is a single-start, coarse, nominal 60-degree metric helix with flattened roots and crests. Left-handed selection changes only the bore and preserves the two-direction diamond knurl. Hidden threads render a smooth bore at minor diameter. The mounting end is Z=0 and the other end is Z=length; both cut annular faces join the outer profile directly to the bore.

The indexed surface is a single connected closed genus-one mesh, outward oriented. No intersecting cylinder overlays or decorative independent knurl strips are used. Dimensions, thread sizes, wall guards and repeat limits remain owned by modelprinter.

`radialSegments` defaults to the first multiple of eight times the tooth count that is at least 96. It must be within 24–768 and divisible by four times the tooth count. `segmentsPerPitch` defaults to 24 and must be an integer within 8–64. Before sampling, the renderer rejects estimated allocations above 400,000 vertices. Tests check closure, edge winding, connectedness, Euler characteristic, nondegenerate triangles, positive volume, all supported thread sizes, exact end planes, crest/root/bore radii, half-pitch crest shifts, bore variation, independent handedness, smooth-bore mode, wall guards, allocation guards and React/vanilla bounds and volumes. A standard labeled ISOMETRIC/TOP/FRONT/SIDE PNG shows the complete string and brass geometry.
