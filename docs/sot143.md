# SOT143B

Nominal physical dimensions follow [Nexperia SOT143B](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/PRTR5V0U2X.pdf), package outline: 2.9 × 1.3 mm body, 1.0 mm overall height, 2.3 mm nominal lead span, 0.43 mm ordinary leads and 0.83 mm pin 1 lead. The pin-1 center is offset relative to the other three leads.

`SOT143` and `Footprinter3d footprint="sot143"` use physical lead sizes instead of extruding the larger solder lands. Numbered pad locations determine orientation. A larger custom pad pitch is not evidence for a larger SOT143 body.

Snapshot columns are isometric, top, underside, front, side, rear isometric. Each fixture is a single six-view image, including transparent reference pads.
