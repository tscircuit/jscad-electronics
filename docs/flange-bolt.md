# FlangeBolt renderer

`FlangeBolt`, `createFlangeBoltGeom` and `createFlangeBoltMesh` accept modelprinter's exported `FlangeBoltModelPropsInput`. Geometry consumes `flangeboltModelPropsSchema` directly; dimension tables, units, defaults and validation live in modelprinter. The component also accepts a color. React, vanilla and footprint dispatch all produce the same mechanical geometry with `pads: "none"`.

The complete example is `flangebolt_headstandard(iso4162)_m6_l25mm_fullthread_plainface`. `headstandard` identifies ISO 4162:2012's small-series head/flange envelope only: full threading extends the standard's thread length. This preserves the intended fully threaded model without claiming full ISO conformity. The M6 hex is 8 mm across-flats, the flange is 13.6 mm in diameter and the entire head is 6.8 mm high.

The indexed mesh is one welded, outward-oriented, closed surface. It includes a single-start helix of the nominal flattened 60-degree profile, a clipped terminal chamfer, a tangent circular under-head fillet, a plain flange bearing face, a conical flange shoulder, regular hexagonal wrench flats and a conical top chamfer. The bearing datum is Z=0; the tip is Z=-length and the head top is Z=headHeight. Handedness and phase follow modelprinter. Hidden threads retain major diameter and all other features. The flange shoulder and head chamfer are sampled separately from the thread. Flange edge rounding and hex-to-flange fillets are deliberately omitted as documented nominal choices.

`radialSegments` defaults to 96 and must be divisible by 24 within 24–192. `threadStepsPerTurn` defaults to 24 within 24–96. Meshes exceeding 400,000 sampled vertices fail before allocation. Tests verify every supported size, edge incidence and opposite edge winding, connectedness, Euler characteristic, nondegenerate triangles, positive signed volume, dimensions, tangent blend, helical phase, handedness, hidden threads, resolution limits and matching React/vanilla routing. The checked-in PNG uses the shared standard four-view renderer and labels the complete string. Modelprinter's companion contract documentation identifies the primary source figures and table provenance.
